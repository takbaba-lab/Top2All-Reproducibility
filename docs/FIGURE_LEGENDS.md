# Figure legends

## Figure 1. Sparse-to-transcriptome reconstruction framework and evaluation design.
(A) Overview of Top2All. For each sample, a sparse profile comprising the nominal Top1000 highest-expressed genes is used as input to reconstruct a transcriptome-wide expression profile.
(B) Benchmark design comprising 24 mouse adrenal zona fasciculata (zF) cell-number-normalized RNA-seq (CNN RNA-seq) profiles from eight biological conditions, with three biological replicates per condition. Rows show male and female conditions, and columns indicate biological replicate numbers. Male conditions were Sham, Cas+Oil, Cas+DHT, and Cas+E2; female conditions were Sham, Ovx+Oil, Ovx+DHT, and Ovx+E2. Cas, castration; Ovx, ovariectomy; DHT, dihydrotestosterone; E2, 17β-estradiol.
(C) Validation schemes. Replicate-wise out-of-fold (OOF) evaluation holds out one replicate from each condition in each of three folds, giving 16 training and eight test samples per fold; Top2All is evaluated both with and without explicit condition labels. Leave-one-condition-out (LOCO) evaluation holds out all three replicates of one condition in each of eight folds, giving 21 training and three test samples per fold, without condition labels. Gray and orange circles indicate training and test samples, respectively; green symbols indicate whether condition labels are supplied.
(D) Complementary evaluation levels comprising sample-wise reconstruction, gene-wise reconstruction, replicate-specific residuals, and unseen-condition shifts. The gene universe contains 24,410 genes. The union of the nominal sample-specific Top1000 gene sets contains 1,312 genes, leaving 23,098 fixed non-Top1000 genes. Of these, 6,951 show nonzero standard deviation of measured log1p expression across the 24 samples. Formal reconstruction evaluations use predictions before any overwrite of observed input genes.
## Figure 2. High sample-wise correlation overestimates reconstruction performance.
(A) Sample-wise Pearson correlations between predicted and measured log1p expression across 23,098 fixed non-Top1000 genes for Top2All, GlobalMean, and SameConditionMean. Each jittered point represents one of 24 OOF test samples; black horizontal segments indicate medians. GlobalMean predicts the mean log1p expression of the 16 training samples in the corresponding fold, whereas SameConditionMean uses the two training replicates from the test sample's condition.
(B) Gene-wise Pearson correlations and (C) gene-wise mean absolute errors (MAEs; log1p units) for the same methods, evaluated across 24 samples for each of 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes. Boxes span the interquartile range (IQR), black lines indicate medians, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 IQR of the box. Outlier points are omitted from display, but all genes contribute to the distributions. Panels A–C use condition-aware Top2All with log1p/scale seeds 42/1234.
(D) Median sample-wise versus median gene-wise Pearson correlation under strict fold-local OOF evaluation. Blue circles show separate no-condition Top2All models with seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236; orange squares, yellow triangles, and pink diamonds denote retrieval using the nearest one, three, or five training profiles (kNN1, kNN3, and kNN5), respectively. Strict targets exclude both the training-fold Top1000 union and each test sample's observed Top1000 genes, yielding 23,111–23,136 eligible genes per test sample. Gene-wise summaries are calculated for the 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes, all of which are eligible in all 24 held-out samples and therefore satisfy the prespecified minimum-observation criterion. Retrieval predicts the mean log1p expression profile of the selected training neighbors. All reference profiles and baseline means are derived exclusively from the corresponding training fold.
## Figure 3. Condition labels do not consistently improve reconstruction beyond the sparse expression profile.
(A) Median gene-wise Pearson and Spearman correlations for OOF Top2All predictions with and without explicit condition labels. Open circles indicate no-condition models and filled circles indicate condition-aware models; lines connect models within each computational seed pair. Blue, orange, and green identify log1p/scale seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236, respectively. Seed pairs represent computational training replicates rather than biological replicates.
(B) Corresponding paired median gene-wise root mean square error (RMSE), mean absolute error (MAE), and R-squared (R²), shown in separate subpanels. Panels A and B summarize 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes, with each gene evaluated across 24 OOF samples; RMSE and MAE are expressed in log1p units.
(C) Fraction of retrieved neighbors sharing the test sample's biological condition when sparse Top1000 profiles alone were used to select k = 1, 3, or 5 neighbors from the corresponding OOF training fold, without using condition labels. Fractions are pooled over 24 test samples, corresponding to 24, 72, and 120 selected-neighbor assignments for k = 1, 3, and 5, respectively. Gray circles connected by a solid line show the observed fractions; the dashed line indicates the chance reference of 2/16 = 1/8 = 0.125, because two of the 16 training samples belong to the same biological condition as each held-out sample.
## Figure 4. Top2All captures condition-associated transcriptomic structure but poorly reconstructs replicate-specific variation.
(A) Sample-wise Pearson correlations between measured and predicted OOF replicate residuals across 23,098 fixed non-Top1000 genes. For each held-out sample, a condition-level reference was defined as the mean log1p expression profile of the two same-condition training replicates. The measured residual and predicted residual were calculated by subtracting this same training-derived reference from the held-out measured and predicted profiles, respectively. Each point represents one of 24 test samples; open and filled blue circles indicate no-condition and condition-aware Top2All, respectively, using log1p/scale seeds 42/1234. Black horizontal segments indicate medians.
(B) Overall mean absolute error (MAE) to held-out measured expression for SameConditionMean, no-condition Top2All, and condition-aware Top2All, using the same Top2All seed pair as in A. Each value is calculated across 23,098 genes and 24 held-out samples, corresponding to 554,352 gene-sample pairs. This panel reports prediction error relative to measured log1p expression, not residual MAE. Green filled, blue open, and blue filled circles denote SameConditionMean, no-condition Top2All, and condition-aware Top2All, respectively.
(C) Median gene-wise Pearson and Spearman correlations between measured and predicted LOCO within-condition residuals across 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes, with each gene evaluated across 24 held-out samples. For each biological condition, measured expression profiles were centered on the mean of the three measured replicates, whereas predicted profiles were independently centered on the mean of the corresponding three predictions. This centering was performed only for evaluation and did not provide condition information to the model. Lines connect the Pearson and Spearman summaries within each computational seed pair, and the dashed horizontal line marks zero correlation.
(D) Ratio of predicted to measured LOCO residual standard deviation, calculated using population standard deviation over the pooled 554,352 residual entries from 23,098 genes and 24 samples. In C and D, blue, orange, and green denote no-condition Top2All seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236, respectively; each point represents one computational seed-pair summary.
## Figure 5. Reference retrieval provides a stronger baseline than learned reconstruction for both unseen conditions and held-out replicates.
(A) LOCO centroid-shift summaries for no-condition Top2All. For each held-out condition, measured and predicted condition shifts were defined as the respective mean profiles of its three test replicates relative to the mean measured log1p profile of the 21 training samples. Shift slope is the regression slope of predicted versus measured condition shifts, fitted with an intercept across 23,098 fixed non-Top1000 genes; SD ratio is the ratio of predicted to measured condition-shift standard deviation across the same genes. Each point represents the median over the eight held-out conditions. Blue, orange, and green indicate log1p/scale seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236, respectively.
(B) LOCO retrieval comparison showing median Pearson correlation between predicted and measured condition shifts (upper subpanel) and median condition-shift MAE in log1p units (lower subpanel) across the same eight held-out conditions and 23,098 genes. Top2All corresponds to the prespecified seed pair 42/1234. Retrieval predictions are generated by averaging the measured full log1p expression profiles of the nearest one, three, or five training samples selected using the sparse Top1000 profiles. The Zero shift control predicts the training centroid and is included only in the MAE subpanel because its shift Pearson correlation is undefined.
(C) Median gene-wise Pearson correlation and (D) median gene-wise MAE under strict fold-local OOF evaluation. Eligible prediction targets exclude both the training-fold Top1000 union and the observed Top1000 genes of each held-out sample. Gene-wise summaries are calculated for 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes; all retained genes are eligible in all 24 held-out samples and therefore satisfy the prespecified minimum-observation criterion. Separate blue circles show no-condition Top2All models with seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236. In B–D, orange squares, yellow triangles, and pink diamonds denote kNN1, kNN3, and kNN5, respectively; gray circles in B denote Zero shift. Neighbor selection and all reference expression profiles are restricted to the corresponding training fold. MAE is expressed in log1p units. Points summarize conditions or genes as specified above and do not represent uncertainty intervals.
## Figure S1. Reconstruction performance varies with target-gene variability.
(A) Median gene-wise Pearson correlation and (B) median gene-wise mean absolute error (MAE; log1p units) across quartiles of target-gene variability for 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes. Each gene is evaluated across 24 OOF samples. Quartiles were defined exclusively from the standard deviation (SD) of measured log1p expression across the 24 samples, using sample SD (ddof = 1), without using predictions for binning. Q1–Q4 contain 1,738, 1,738, 1,737, and 1,738 genes, respectively, ordered from lowest to highest measured-expression SD. Light-blue thin lines with small circles show individual condition-aware Top2All models for log1p/scale seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236. The dark-blue thick line with larger circles shows the unweighted median of the three seed-specific within-quartile metric medians. Gray and green lines denote GlobalMean and SameConditionMean, respectively, calculated from the corresponding OOF training samples as in Figure 2. Lines connect the ordered variability quartiles for visualization; individual seed curves may overlap the three-seed median. The same measured-data-defined quartiles are used for all methods.
## Figure S2. Performance across three prespecified computational seed pairs.
(A) Median gene-wise Pearson correlation and (B) median gene-wise mean absolute error (MAE; log1p units) for condition-aware OOF, no-condition OOF, no-condition LOCO, and strict fold-local no-condition OOF evaluation. Blue, orange, and green points represent computational log1p/scale seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236, respectively, with small horizontal offsets within each categorical setting. Black horizontal segments indicate the unweighted median of the three seed-specific summaries; no lines connect settings or seeds.
Condition-aware OOF, no-condition OOF, and LOCO summaries use 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes, with each gene evaluated across 24 held-out samples. No-condition OOF and strict fold-local OOF use the same underlying predictions but different target-selection protocols: the former uses the global fixed non-Top1000 target set, whereas the latter additionally excludes the training-fold Top1000 union and each held-out sample's observed Top1000 genes and requires at least 12 eligible observations per gene. In this dataset, the retained gene-wise evaluation set for strict OOF also comprises the same 6,951 genes, each with 24 eligible observations, resulting in nearly identical summaries. These two settings therefore represent different evaluation protocols applied to the same predictions and should not be interpreted as independent validation datasets. Seed pairs are computational training replicates and are distinct from biological replicates and validation folds.
## Figure S3. Reconstruction performance is non-monotonic with input depth.
(A) Median gene-wise Pearson correlation and (B) median gene-wise mean absolute error (MAE; log1p units) for no-condition OOF Top2All across nominal TopN input depths of 100, 200, 300, 500, 1000, and 2000. For this common-target analysis, all input depths are evaluated on the same 5,605 true-variable genes outside the union of the Top2000 gene sets across all 24 samples, ensuring that the evaluation targets remain unobserved at every input depth.
(C) Median gene-wise Pearson correlation and (D) median gene-wise MAE for a bridge analysis using the same 6,951 true-variable fixed non-Top1000 genes as the main Top1000 benchmark, evaluated at Top100, Top200, Top300, Top500, and Top1000. Top2000 is not included in the bridge analysis because its observed input gene set would overlap the fixed non-Top1000 evaluation target; its omission is therefore intentional rather than missing data.
In both analyses, true-variable genes are defined as genes with positive standard deviation of measured log1p expression across the 24 samples, using sample SD (ddof = 1), and target-gene identities are held fixed across input depths and computational seed pairs. Gene-wise metrics are calculated across the 24 OOF samples. Blue, orange, and green thin lines show log1p/scale seed pairs 42/1234, 43/1235, and 44/1236, respectively; the thick black line shows the unweighted median of the three seed-specific median gene-wise metrics at each input depth. Lines connect ordered input settings, which are displayed at equal spacing. TopN denotes the nominal depth of the ranked input-gene list; the number of retained tokenizable genes can therefore be smaller than the nominal TopN.
